Twenty new questions that add to the 40-question practice midterm. They cover every topic on the midterm review slide: mapping, concept mapping, Lynchian analysis, walking, cycling, transport and streets, and plans (Lectures 2-1 to 4-2). Questions follow your professor’s style: “Which statement below best describes…” and short-option “Which … below…” questions. None of them repeat a question from the practice midterm, and each has one clearly correct answer. Pick an answer and you’ll see right away whether it’s right, plus a short explanation of where it comes from in the lectures.
